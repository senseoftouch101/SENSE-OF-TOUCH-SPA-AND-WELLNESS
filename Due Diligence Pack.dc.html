5. Market & competition

Tema is a port city and residential hub in Greater Accra with middle- and upper-income households, corporate employers and motorway access from Accra. Sense of Touch targets the space between basic salons and hotel spas: hotel-grade experience and hygiene at neighbourhood accessibility, with an African-inspired identity.

11. Impact & ESG

  • Jobs and skills: 8 roles in Year 1 rising to 12 (plan); the Institute creates a training pathway into professional beauty and wellness work.

  • Safety standards: written protocols, consent, records and FDA-compliant products in a category where informal practice is common.
