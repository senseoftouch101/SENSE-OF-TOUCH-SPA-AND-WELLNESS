THE PROBLEM

Ghana's fastest-growing consumers are spending on self-care — and getting gambled on.

Unregulated products on the skin. Untrained hands on the body. No standards, no records, no accountability. The only safe option is a hotel spa built for tourists — not for people who want care every month.

WHAT IT COSTS

Every month this goes unsolved, three things are lost.

01The client's skin and moneyRepeat spend on treatments that don't work and products that damage. Trust burned, visit after visit.
02The category's valuePremium skincare spend flows to imported brands — many built on African ingredients like shea and black soap.
03The windowNo local brand owns premium-accessible wellness. The first one to earn trust at scale keeps it. If it isn't us, it's a foreign entrant.
THE POSITION

The African-inspired wellness brand that owns both the ritual and the product.

Hotel-grade care at a local price. The spa earns the trust; our own lines — The Lip Bar and AFTERCARE — capture the margin.
