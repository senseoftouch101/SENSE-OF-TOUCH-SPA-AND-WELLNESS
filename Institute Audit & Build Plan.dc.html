Audit & build plan

Learn the Art. Master the Touch. Transform Your Future.

Findings from inspecting the current project against the master brief, the proposed direction, and the build order. Nothing existing has been replaced; the rename to the Institute is the only change applied so far.

1 · What already exists

Brand systemThe full site already uses the exact brand palette (#493126, #536B4E, #C6A15B, #E9DDC9, #F8F4ED, #24221D) with Cormorant Garamond, Montserrat and Allura. No new design system is needed.
3D enginePlain Three.js (no build step), loaded lazily, each module with WebGL detection, reduced-motion handling and a static fallback: splash3d.js 3D logo reveal · hero3d.js water ripple, floating leaves, gold particles · scene3d.js leaves, stones, lip, voucher accents · product3d.js interactive AFTERCARE product viewer.
Motion systemmotion.js: scroll reveal, image mask reveal, parallax, nav state, page transitions, card tilt, floating CTA — reduced-motion aware. The Institute pages don’t load it yet.
PlatformShared sign-in (sot-auth.js), shared store (sot-store.js), client CRM, Admin console — all browser-storage prototypes, designed to move to Supabase with row-level security.
Institute modulePublic site, apply/track/verify, student + instructor portal, LMS, hybrid workflow, practicals, certificates with QR, payments with verification, communications queue, admin with roles and audit log (academy-core.js).

4 · Design & 3D direction

Following your recommendation: immersive where people browse, calm where people act.

ImmersiveHome (short 3D logo reveal with Skip, shown once per visit; hero with floating stones, pure leaves and oil droplets that respond to the cursor), course catalogue (tilting cards, 3D course objects per department), virtual tour.
RestrainedAbout, instructors, admissions, events: scroll reveals, parallax photography, gentle background motion only.
Fast & plainRegistration, payments, portals, admin: no WebGL. Only micro-motion — animated step progress, success/error states, progress rings and dashboard charts.
TechnologyKeep the existing plain Three.js modules and motion.js instead of adding React Three Fiber and GSAP: same results, no build step, smaller downloads on Ghanaian mobile networks. GLB models from Blender (Draco-compressed) plug into the same loader.
Virtual tourBuilt from real photographs of the spa, with hotspots and labels. Every space is tagged Actual facility or Proposed / concept; a plain list with photos and descriptions is the accessible alternative.
Learning illustrationsStylised, labelled models (facial zones, back massage stroke directions, wood-tool movement), each marked “illustrative — not a medical or diagnostic tool”.
Performance3D only starts when visible and on capable devices; low-power and reduced-motion users get photography. Every page works fully without WebGL.

CPractical evaluation forms with ratings and Pass / Resit / Pending; instructor quiz and assignment builders; animated progress in the student portal.
DFacilities & virtual tour and 3D learning illustrations (needs your photos / models).
EAdmin: events & workshops, Institute content management, dashboard charts, financial reports by period.
LaunchDeveloper phases outside this prototype: Supabase + RLS, Paystack/Hubtel webhooks, Zoom/Meet, email/SMS/WhatsApp providers, security, accessibility and device testing, deployment.

6 · Honest status

Everything here is a working prototype in the browser. No payment provider, messaging provider, video host, live classroom, database or deployment is connected or verified yet. No accreditation is claimed. Fees, durations, intakes and statistics are shown only once you enter real data.
