Cedi & importsLocal suppliers, quarterly price reviews, buffer stock and own-brand products.
Power & waterGenerator or solar backup and water storage are in the start-up budget.
Staff turnoverFair pay, retail commission and a training pathway through the Institute.
Client safetyProtocols, consultation, consent and insurance. All treatments are non-invasive.
Key personSpa Manager role, written SOPs and systems that run without the founder in the room.
