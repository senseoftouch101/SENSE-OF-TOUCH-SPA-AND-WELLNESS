Questions

Investor FAQ

What kind of partners are you looking for?

Lenders, development-finance institutions, asset and supplier financiers, sponsors and brand partners, and companies interested in corporate wellness. The founder currently prefers to retain full ownership.

Are the financial figures forecasts?

No. They are planning assumptions for the flagship spa, built from a small set of inputs you can adjust in the interactive financial model. The Institute and own-brand products are upside and are not included.

Why do some documents need an NDA?

The business plan, financial model, growth deck and due diligence pack contain commercially sensitive information. You accept the confidentiality agreement once, inside the investor room.

Can I visit the spa?

Yes. Tick “arrange a call or visit” on your request, or use the meeting request in the investor room. We are at Estate Junction, Community 25, Tema.

How is the business structured?

Sense of Touch LLC in the United States, with a Ghana operating entity for the spa, Lip Bar and Institute. Full details are in the due diligence pack.
