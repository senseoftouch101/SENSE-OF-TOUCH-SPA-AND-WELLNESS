Complete Spa Operations & Management System

Paste everything below the line into Claude Code at the root of the production repository. The working prototype is the Front desk tab in Admin; this brief turns it into a real, multi-user system on Supabase.

0. Role and goal

You are the lead engineer for SENSE OF TOUCH SPA & WELLNESS (Tema, Community 25, Ghana). Build the Spa Operations System: one connected workflow from enquiry to next appointment, covering booking, reception, therapist scheduling, consultation and treatment records, payments and cash-up, retail (THE LIP BAR, AFTERCARE), aftercare and follow-up, CRM, audit and founder reporting. It must share one client database with the spa, store and Sense of Touch Institute. Never create a second customer table.

Work in phases. At the end of each phase: run the tests, list what changed, list open questions, stop for review. Do not invent prices, policies or medical claims. Leave a clearly marked TODO for the founder instead.

  • sot-store.js: bookings, sales/checkout, gift cards, SOT Rewards, notifications, daily job. sot-auth.js: role list (RD) and Admin tab permissions. sot-supabase.js and Connect Supabase: current cloud wiring.
  • Consultation.dc.html (intake), CRM.dc.html, Operations.dc.html (therapist schedule, suppliers, invoices), Executive Dashboard.dc.html, Shop.dc.html (product catalogue).
  • Brand: Cormorant Garamond + Montserrat; ink #24221D, brown #493126, gold #C6A15B, sage #536B4E, cream #F8F4ED / #F3EEE6, line #E9DDC9, error #9A3B2A. Square corners, 1px lines, uppercase micro-labels. Keep the existing visual language.

2. Stack and conventions

  • Supabase: Postgres, Auth, Row Level Security on every table, Edge Functions (Deno) for payments, messaging and scheduled jobs, Storage for consent PDFs.
  • Frontend: keep the current page set and design. Replace localStorage reads and writes with a typed data layer (one module per domain) that calls Supabase. Keep an offline queue for reception so a dropped connection never loses a check-in or payment.
  • Currency GHS stored as integer pesewas. Timezone Africa/Accra (GMT, no DST). Phones normalised to E.164 +233.
  • Every write goes through an RPC or Edge Function that also writes audit_log. No direct table writes from the browser for money, treatment records or permissions.

3. Roles and permissions

Roles are those in sot-auth.js plus therapist. Enforce in RLS, not just the UI.

  • Receptionist: calendar, bookings, walk-ins, waitlist, check-in, consent tick, deposits, checkout, cash-up for own shift, feedback logging. Cannot read treatment notes. Cannot refund above a limit set by the manager.
  • Therapist: own schedule, own clients' intake and treatment records (read/write for own visits, read for prior visits of the same client), stage changes to In treatment and Ready to pay. No payments.
  • Department/Spa manager: everything above, all treatment records, refunds, day close approval, staff schedules, audit view.
  • Director/Founder, General admin, Super admin: all, plus reporting, integrations and settings. Finance: payments, cash-ups, reports; no treatment notes. Auditor: read-only reports and audit log; no notes.
  • Treatment notes visibility: therapist + manager only. They never appear in marketing exports, CRM segments or the customer portal.

4. Database schema

Create migrations for these tables (uuid ids, created_at, updated_at, created_by on all):

  • clients: phone (unique), name, email, birthday_mmdd, marketing_consent, source, notes_public. Shared with store and Institute.
  • services: name, category (Massage, Wood Therapy, Facial & Skin, AFTERCARE, The Lip Bar), duration_min, buffer_min, price_pesewas (nullable until confirmed), deposit_rule, rebook_days, aftercare_template_id, active.
  • therapists (staff_id, services[]), rooms (name, kind, services[]), staff_shifts (staff_id, date, start, end, type: shift/leave/blocked).
  • appointments: client_id, service_id, therapist_id, room_id, starts_at, ends_at, status (requested, confirmed, cancelled, no_show, completed), stage (booked, arrived, consultation, in_treatment, ready_to_pay, completed), source (web, front_desk, walk_in, whatsapp), deposit_pesewas, deposit_outcome, cancel_reason, late_cancel, consent_confirmed_at, arrived_at, started_at, finished_at, aftercare_sent_at. Exclusion constraints so a therapist or room can never be double-booked (tstzrange + btree_gist).
  • waitlist: client_id, service_id, date, window, therapist_pref, status (waiting, slot_opened, booked, removed), offered_appointment_id.
  • intake_forms: client_id, answers jsonb, contraindications[], signed_at, signature_path, version. consents: appointment_id, form_version, confirmed_by.
  • treatment_records (private schema, separate RLS): appointment_id, client_id, therapist_id, areas, products_used, technique, observations, reactions, advice, concerns[], edited_history jsonb.
  • payments: appointment_id/sale_id, kind (deposit, sale, refund), method (momo, card, cash, gift_card), amount_pesewas, provider_ref, status, received_by. sales + sale_items (service or retail, product_id, qty, price). cash_ups: date, shift, expected jsonb, counted jsonb, variance, note, closed_by, approved_by.
  • products, stock_movements (sale, return, restock, adjustment, batch, expiry). Retail sold at checkout decrements stock.
  • notifications: client_id, channel, template, body, deliver_on, status (queued, due, sent, failed), provider_ref, related_id.
  • feedback: client_id, appointment_id, kind (review, complaint, suggestion), rating, comment, status (logged, needs_recovery, resolved), recovery_action, resolved_by.
  • audit_log: actor_id, actor_role, action, entity, entity_id, before jsonb, after jsonb, ip, at. Append-only (no update/delete grants).
  • integration_checks: name, ok, detail, checked_at. backups: taken_at, location, restore_tested_at.

5. Workflows (match the prototype)

  1. Live calendar: day view by therapist or by room, 30-minute grid 09:00–19:00, now-line, colour by stage, dashed outline for unconfirmed web requests. Tap an empty slot to prefill a booking. Week view and drag-to-reschedule come later.
  2. Booking: clash check on therapist and room, respecting shifts, leave and service buffers. Confirm by WhatsApp.
  3. Walk-in: pick a service and get the first free therapist and room from now. Create the appointment already at the Arrived stage.
  4. Waitlist: when a booking is cancelled or marked no-show, flag the first person waiting that day. Booking them from the waitlist sends an offer message.
  5. Deposits: record MoMo, card or cash with a reference. Over 24h before the start a cancellation means "refund due". Inside 24h, or a no-show, the deposit is kept. Refunds are logged as negative payments. Policy values live in settings (TODO: founder to confirm).
  6. Check-in → consultation: show the intake status. Treatment cannot start until consent is confirmed. Prompt for an intake form if none is on file or it is older than 12 months.
  7. Treatment record: therapist writes areas, products, technique, observations, reactions, advice and client concerns. Every edit is versioned.
  8. Ready to pay → checkout: suggest products from the concerns (Product Routine Builder, approved product info only, show "not a diagnosis"). Hand the service and chosen retail to the existing checkout, which already settles deposit, gift card, reward code and the rest. Completing the sale completes the appointment and awards points.
  9. Aftercare: one tap queues three messages: aftercare text today, a 1–5 feedback request in 2 days, and a rebook nudge at the service's rebook_days.
  10. Cash-up: expected per method from sales, deposits and refunds; staff enter counted amounts. Any variance needs a note. Manager approval locks the day.
  11. Feedback centre: ratings of 3 or below and all complaints open a service-recovery task, closed with the action taken.

6. Client-facing differentiators

  • Digital Spa Passport (staff): visits, client-since date, points, active gift cards, next visit, service mix, no-shows, intake status, and treatment records for permitted roles only.
  • My Wellness Journey (customer portal, Account page): own visit history, approved aftercare per visit, products bought, rebook button. Never shows therapist notes.
  • Product Routine Builder: concern → approved products for THE LIP BAR and AFTERCARE. Rules live in a table the founder edits. No medical claims.
  • Customer Feedback Centre: as above, plus the WhatsApp 1–5 reply flowing in automatically.
  • Integration status monitor: database, WhatsApp, Paystack (MoMo and card), Google review link, daily automations, backups. Each shows working / check / not set, from scheduled checks.

7. Integrations

  • Paystack for MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money and cards: Edge Function to initialise, webhook to confirm (verify signature, idempotent on reference). A payment counts only after the webhook.
  • WhatsApp Business Cloud API: approved templates for confirmation, reminder (24h), reschedule, waitlist offer, aftercare, feedback and rebook. Inbound replies go to the Admin WhatsApp inbox. Fall back to SMS when delivery fails.
  • Email (Resend or similar) for receipts and staff alerts. Google review link after checkout, never tied to rewards.
  • Scheduled Edge Function at 06:00 GMT replacing SOT.runDaily: release queued messages, expire gift cards, birthday points, 24h reminders, integration checks, nightly backup.

8. Security, privacy, audit, backup

  • Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843): consent for marketing kept separate from treatment consent. Clients can request export and deletion; treatment records are kept for the retention period set by the founder (TODO).
  • Treatment records and intake answers in a private schema with column-level grants. Access to them is itself logged.
  • Audit log is append-only and covers bookings, moves, cancellations, deposits, refunds, stage changes, record edits, price changes, permission changes, day close and exports.
  • Staff sessions: idle timeout, per-device revoke, 2FA for managers and above.
  • Backups: Supabase PITR plus a nightly encrypted export to a separate bucket. Do a monthly restore drill into staging and record it in backups.restore_tested_at.

9. Founder reporting

Feed Executive Dashboard from views: revenue by day, week and month split by service and retail; deposits held; refunds; cash-up variances; bookings new vs returning; no-show and late-cancel rates; therapist and room utilisation; average wait from arrival to start; retail attach rate per visit; routine-builder conversion; feedback average and open recoveries; Lip Bar and AFTERCARE stock and margin.

11. Tests (realistic scenarios, must pass)

  • Two receptionists book the same therapist at 10:30 at the same moment: exactly one succeeds.
  • Walk-in at 16:45 for a 90-minute service with close at 19:00: offered 17:00 or later, never past close.
  • Cancel 30h ahead with a GHS 100 MoMo deposit → refund due → refunded → cash-up expected drops by 100.
  • Cancel 3h ahead → deposit kept; waitlisted client for that day flagged and offered.
  • Therapist cannot start treatment without consent; receptionist cannot read treatment notes through the UI, the API or an export.
  • Checkout with deposit + partial gift card + reward code + MoMo for the rest; Paystack webhook replayed twice → one payment.
  • Retail sale decrements stock and awards retail points; return reverses both.
  • Aftercare queues 3 messages with correct dates; a failed WhatsApp send retries, then falls back to SMS.
  • Rating 2 opens a recovery task; resolving it records who and what.
  • Cash-up with a GHS 20 variance cannot close without a note; after approval the day is locked.
  • Every action above appears in audit_log with actor and before/after.
  • Restore last night's backup into staging; record counts match.

12. Deployment

  • Environments: local (supabase start), staging, production; migrations via CI only. Secrets in Supabase and host env, never in the client.
  • Paystack and WhatsApp in test mode on staging; go-live checklist flips them after a full scripted rehearsal with real staff.
  • Monitoring: Edge Function errors, webhook failures and queued messages older than 24h raise an alert to the manager.
  • Deliver a short staff guide per role (receptionist, therapist, manager) with screenshots.
