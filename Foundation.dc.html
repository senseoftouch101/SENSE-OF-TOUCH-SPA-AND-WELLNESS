Our purpose

Every touch tells a story of care

Talent for care is everywhere; access to training, safe products and good information is not. The Foundation exists to close that gap — so more people can earn a living through skilled, professional work, and more communities can look after their wellbeing safely.

It works alongside the spa and the Sense of Touch Institute, using their trainers, rooms and standards, while keeping its own funds, decisions and reporting separate.

Programmes

Four ways we help

01ScholarshipsFull and part tuition at the Sense of Touch Institute for students with talent and limited means, including kits and transport support.
02Skills for women & youthShort practical courses with business and entrepreneurship training, so graduates can work professionally or start their own business.
03Community wellness daysFree self-care sessions and relaxation for communities, carers and frontline workers in Tema and beyond.
04Safe beauty educationTalks and materials on skin health, hygiene and recognising unsafe or unregistered products, for schools and community groups.
