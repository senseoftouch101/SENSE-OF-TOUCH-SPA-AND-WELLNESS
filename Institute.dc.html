About the Institute

A training division of Sense of Touch Spa & Wellness

Our vision. To develop professional skills, encourage entrepreneurship and prepare students for opportunities in the beauty, spa and wellness industry.

The Institute teaches the same standards of hygiene, consultation and care that guide our spa. Students learn from working practitioners and practise in real treatment rooms in Tema.

We are honest about what our certificates mean: they record that you completed an Institute course and met its assessed requirements. They are not, at present, accredited or nationally recognised qualifications.
Mission

To develop professional skills, encourage entrepreneurship and prepare students for opportunities in the beauty, spa and wellness industry.

Vision

An international standard of beauty and wellness education with a distinctive African identity — rooted in Tema, open to the world.

Values

Care before technique · Hygiene and safety, always · Honest assessment · Respect and consent · Enterprise and independence.

Admissions

Requirements, process and tuition

Admission requirements

Each course lists its own entry requirements. Most foundation courses are planned for beginners aged 18 and over. You’ll tell us about your education, any experience and your goals — there’s no exam to enter.

We only ask for an identification document where a course genuinely requires it, and store it privately.

Tuition and payment

Fees are published for each intake and confirmed in your offer. Pay by Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) or card. Where an intake offers instalments, the plan is shown in your offer.

Your place is confirmed only once our finance team verifies the payment with the provider — keep your transaction ID.
