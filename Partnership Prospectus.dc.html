Who we are

Professional care, rooted in Africa

Sense of Touch is a Ghanaian spa, beauty and wellness group founded by Woselande Carmelle Theus. We combine professional treatments under written protocols with an African-inspired identity, our own brands — The Lip Bar and AFTERCARE — and an Institute that trains the next generation of beauty and wellness professionals.

Spa & WellnessMassage, wood therapy, facials, body, scalp and lip care in Tema.
The InstituteIn-person, live online, self-paced and hybrid training with practical assessment.
The Lip BarA dedicated lip-care concept with non-invasive treatments.
AFTERCAREAfrican-inspired skincare, launching after FDA Ghana registration.

Why partner with us

  • Skills and jobs: every scholarship and placement leads to practical, employable skills.
  • Safer care: we promote hygiene, consent and safe products in a sector where informal practice is common.
  • Women’s entrepreneurship: founder-led, with business and entrepreneurship training built into our courses.
  • A trusted local brand: visible to clients, students and communities across Tema and Greater Accra.
