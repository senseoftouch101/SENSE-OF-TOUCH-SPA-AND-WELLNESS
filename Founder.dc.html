Her story

Woselande Carmelle Theus is the founder of Sense of Touch Spa & Wellness, a luxury African-inspired beauty and wellness destination.

As a Massage Therapist, Aesthetician and Wood Therapy Specialist, she is passionate about helping people experience relaxation, personalised beauty care and holistic wellness.

Through Sense of Touch, she has created a welcoming environment where individuals of all ages and genders can enjoy professional treatments, reconnect with themselves and make self-care a meaningful part of everyday life.

Her vision is to celebrate African-inspired beauty and wellness while delivering an exceptional, modern spa experience.

Meet Woselande Carmelle Theus, Massage Therapist, Aesthetician & Wood Therapy Specialist

We had the good fortune of connecting with Woselande Carmelle Theus, and we’ve shared our conversation below.

The question

Hi Woselande Carmelle, what was your thought process behind starting your own business?

“I knew I had to begin somewhere — one touch at a time.”

As a massage therapist, aesthetician, and wood therapy specialist, my thought process in starting my own business was fuelled by a desire for freedom. I craved not just financial freedom but also emotional, temporal, spiritual, and professional freedom. What initially inspired me was a deep sense of anger, stemming from a lack of affection, love, and care in my own life. I realised that I couldn’t be the only person experiencing this, and I wanted to make a positive impact.

I made a conscious decision to no longer dwell in pain or inflict it upon others. Instead, I wanted to create something that embodied light and love. That’s when I realised the power of touch. Blessed with big hands, I understood the potential of touch as one of our senses. I imagined how different my life could have been if, on those difficult days, someone had simply hugged me and reassured me that everything would be okay. Or if I had been taught about the benefits of massage and the profound relaxation it brings to the mind, body, and soul, helping to release trauma.

“I wanted to create something that embodied light and love. That’s when I realised the power of touch.”

I believe that these experiences would have made a significant difference in my academic performance, my friendships, my relationships with loved ones, and most importantly, my relationship with myself. Growing up in the fast-paced environment of Miami, Florida, and in a Haitian household where everything moves quickly, I recognised the need for a space where individuals could slow down, relax, and find solace. I have vivid memories of my mother coming home and talking non-stop, jumping from one subject to another. Sometimes, it would irritate me, but other times, I would observe her from a distance and wonder what would happen if I simply gave her a hug or rubbed her feet after a long day of school and work. Could that bring peace to our household?

As an entrepreneur, my primary focus was to define the services I offer and the products I wanted to create, all centred around self-love and comfort. Once I realised that God had blessed me with the gift of my hands, along with wisdom, knowledge, and understanding, I knew I could be the solution to problems that others were facing. I wasn’t sure where to start or how far my concept of “sense of touch” would take me, but I knew I had to begin somewhere — one touch at a time — and eventually bring my vision to life.

Through my business, I aim to provide a sanctuary where people can escape the hectic pace of life and rediscover inner peace. I want to offer not only physical rejuvenation but also emotional and spiritual healing. By combining my expertise as a massage therapist, aesthetician, and wood therapy specialist, I am able to create a unique and holistic experience for my clients. Through the power of touch, I can bring about meaningful change and contribute to the overall happiness and fulfillment of those I serve.
